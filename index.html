How it works

  1. OpenTap your two free packs (Adventures and Abstract) once a day in the Packs / Collection tab. They reset at midnight.
  2. CollectFind every swamp character. Trade extra copies for Swamp Coins.
  3. Earn morePlay the Swamp Adventure for Swamp Coins to spend in the shop. Every run also pays Swamp Crystals to dress your own swamp avatar, with no daily limit. Watch X and Discord for bonus codes too.

What is SwampRealms?

SwampRealms is a free card collecting site for the DonK community. Open two free packs every day, collect the whole herd of swamp characters from Common to Mythical, play the Swamp Adventure for Swamp Coins and Swamp Crystals and trade your extra copies. The cards are inspired by DonK, a community meme coin, and are just free fan collectibles for fun.
