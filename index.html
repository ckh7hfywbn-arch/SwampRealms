The story

A star fell into the swamp

Long ago, a fallen star sank into Murkwater and glowed there, green and gold. The frogs called it the Heart. This is what happened to it, and what happens next.

  1. 1
    The Lantern Order

    Frogs gave up sleep to guard the Heart. Every year they fed it three lights: the Ruby from Ember Bog, the Sapphire from under Frostfen and the Moonstone asleep in the ice. A stone Gate tied it to Zaruk, the Realm Warden, who once came through for tea.

  2. 2
    The Red Night

    The Gate turned a thin, cold red. A broken world was leaking through. The Toad Dwarves floated their mountain hall into the clouds, the Hex Sisters bottled the glow in jars, and the Order fell apart, shouting.

  3. 3
    Croaker's promise

    Young Croaker was chosen to seal the Gate. Nobody told him the Heart was tied to it. Year by year the swamp went dim, and giant beasts crept in to drink the leaking light. He has waited by the old camp ever since.

  4. 4
    Then you washed up

    Bare paws, mud in your fur, exactly as the old campfire story said. Dig, build, beat the beasts, gather the three lights and open the Gate again. Zaruk is waiting on the other side.

“Tell the little one there is still tea.”Zaruk, the Realm Warden
