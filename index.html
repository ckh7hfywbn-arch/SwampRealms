What is SwampVerse?

SwampVerse is a free card collecting site for the DonK community. Open a free pack every day, collect the whole herd of swamp characters from Common to Mythical, play Fly Frenzy for Swamp Coins and trade your extra copies. The cards are inspired by DonK, a community meme coin, and are just free fan collectibles for fun.
