How to play

  1. MovementRun with AD or the arrow keys. On touch, use the Left and Right buttons.
  2. JumpPress Space or Jump to leap onto crates and platforms. Hold for a high jump, tap for a short hop.
  3. AttackPress J or E, or Attack, to swing in front of you. The Bramble Bug takes two hits. The Bog Crawler in Level 2 takes three. The Rootmaw boss at the end of Level 3 takes twelve: dodge its ground slam, then strike while it recovers.
  4. CollectiblesGlowspores float along the way. There are 12 in Level 1, 17 in Level 2 and 41 in Level 3. Touch one to collect it. Watch out for thorn vines in Level 3: they hurt.
  5. HealthYou have 3 flames. A Bramble Bug or Bog Crawler touch costs one. Lose them all and it is game over. Lit lanterns save your spot.
  6. GoalReach the goal gate at the far end of Levels 1 and 2. Level 3 ends in a boss fight: defeat the Rootmaw to win.
  7. ScoreGlowspore 100, Bramble Bug 250, Bog Crawler 400, Rootmaw 1,500, finishing a level 1,000, plus a speed bonus of up to 1,000. Your best score for each level is saved.
  8. FragmentsDefeated enemies have a chance to drop a Fragment. Each kind comes from its own enemy, and the Rootmaw drops a rare one. They are saved to your collection straight away.
